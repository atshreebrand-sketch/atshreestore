Refund Policy

Clear, simple guidance for eligible refunds.

When a refund may apply

Refunds may apply to eligible cancellations or approved returns where a refund is offered instead of an exchange. Damaged, incorrect or exceptional cases will be reviewed by our support team.

Cancellation

Cancellation requests should be made as early as possible. Once an order has entered processing or shipping, cancellation may no longer be possible.

Return condition

Eligible returned products must be unworn, unused, unwashed and include original tags, packaging and accessories. Items that fail inspection may be rejected.

Refund method and timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method where supported. Payment-provider processing times may vary after ATSHREE initiates the refund.

Shipping charges

Original shipping charges are generally non-refundable unless the issue is attributable to ATSHREE, such as an incorrect or damaged item.

Damaged or incorrect orders

Contact support@atshree.com promptly with your order number and photographs of the item and packaging.

The policy displayed at the time of purchase applies to that order. ATSHREE may update this page for future orders.
